Privacy Policy

TCMPL AI Onboarding Agent · Last updated 3 November 2026

This policy explains how Talent Carriage ("TCMPL", "we", "us") handles personal data when we use the TCMPL AI Onboarding Agent to help candidates and new employees through joining, on behalf of our client companies. It covers the onboarding web application and our WhatsApp messaging.

1. Data we collect

  • Identity and contact details: name, mobile number, email address.
  • Offer and employment details: role, department, joining date, location, salary and offer terms, manager and team details.
  • Joining documents and form details that a candidate chooses to share, such as identity documents and statutory joining forms.
  • Messages exchanged with the onboarding agent on WhatsApp or in the application, and related delivery information (for example, message time and status).
  • Records of who in the HR team viewed or changed information, and when.

2. How we use it

  • To send offer letters, joining reminders and Day-1 information.
  • To answer candidates' onboarding questions, including with an AI assistant that only uses the client's approved onboarding information.
  • To prepare joining forms and Day-1 readiness for the client's IT, Admin and HR teams.
  • To pass questions to a human recruiter or HR person when needed.

The AI assistant does not negotiate or change any offer terms, and we do not use candidate data for advertising or sell it to anyone.

3. Who we share it with

  • The client company that made the offer, and the TCMPL team handling the onboarding.
  • Service providers that run the service for us: Microsoft Azure (hosting, database, storage and AI processing) and Meta (WhatsApp Business Platform) for message delivery.
  • Authorities, where the law requires it.

4. Storage and security

Data is stored in Microsoft Azure data centres in India, protected by access controls and encryption. Only authorised TCMPL and client staff can sign in to the application.

5. How long we keep it

We keep onboarding data for as long as needed to complete onboarding and meet legal and contractual obligations with the client, and then delete or anonymise it.

6. Your choices and rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 you may ask to access, correct or delete your personal data, withdraw consent, or raise a grievance. You can stop WhatsApp messages at any time by replying "STOP" or by contacting us.
