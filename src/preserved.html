Joining forms

The agent pre-fills the statutory joining forms from the offer, the documents the candidate sent and their WhatsApp answers. The candidate confirms or corrects every field before anything is filed.

Confirmed joining data goes to any HRMS, or stays in TCMPL's database when the client has none.

Day-1 readiness

The agent chases IT, Admin, Security, the manager and payroll on the client's own cadence, records each reply, and escalates anything still open two days before joining.
